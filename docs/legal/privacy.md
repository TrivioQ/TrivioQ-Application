# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 1, 2026 · Effective upon account registration**

Enatos Tech ("we", "us", or "our") operates TrivioQ. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service (the TrivioQ website and mobile application). Please read it carefully. By using TrivioQ, you consent to the practices described here.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Information You Provide Directly

- **Account data:** email address, username, display name, password (stored as a secure hash via Firebase Authentication), date of birth, and profile picture.
- **Preferences:** category interests, difficulty preferences, active notification windows, notification channels (email, web push, mobile push), and target drop frequency you configure in the app.
- **Social data:** friendships you initiate, accept, or block within the TrivioQ community.

### b) Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage data:** trivia answers submitted, questions received, points earned, streaks, hint and reveal-answer usage, on-demand token usage, subscription history (Free, Premium, Plus, Trial), referral tracking (who referred you), and leaderboard activity.
- **Device data:** device push notification token, web push subscription details (browser info), operating system platform (iOS / Android / Web), and general device type — collected to deliver push notifications.
- **Log data:** IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps — collected automatically by our servers and third-party infrastructure for security and operational purposes.

### c) Information from Third-Party Sign-In

If you sign in using Google, we receive your Google account email address and a unique identifier from Google. We do not receive your Google password. Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

### d) Payment Information

If you purchase a Premium or Plus subscription, or buy on-demand tokens, your payment is processed by third-party payment providers (such as Apple App Store, Google Play, or Stripe). We do not collect or store your full credit card details. We only receive confirmation of your payment and subscription status from these providers.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account and authenticate your identity.
- Deliver trivia questions ("Drops") on your chosen schedule, within your category, age rating (All, Teen, Mature), and difficulty preferences.
- Calculate and display scores, streaks, and leaderboard rankings.
- Manage social features, such as processing friend requests and displaying your friends list.
- Administer referral programs, including tracking referrals and awarding free Premium days.
- Administer and track subscription statuses and on-demand token usage.
- Send push notifications (mobile and web) and emails when new Drops are available or for account updates, subject to your notification preferences.
- Respond to support requests and communicate important service updates.
- Monitor and analyse usage patterns to improve the Service, diagnose technical issues, and prevent abuse.
- Enforce our Terms of Service and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising profiling or sell your personal data to third-party advertisers.

## 3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, our legal bases for processing your personal data are:

- **Contract performance** — processing necessary to provide the Service you have signed up for.
- **Legitimate interests** — security monitoring, fraud prevention, product improvement, and service communications, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Consent** — for optional features such as push notifications and emails; you may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation** — where we are required by law to process your data.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service providers:** We use Firebase (Google LLC) for authentication, push notification delivery, and database infrastructure. We also use third-party payment processors. These providers process data only on our behalf and are bound by data processing agreements.
- **Social & Leaderboard data:** Your username, display name, profile picture, and score are visible to other TrivioQ users on the public leaderboard and to users you interact with via our friendship features.
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g. a court or government agency).
- **Business transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. We will provide notice before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. If you request account deletion, your account will enter a pending deletion state for **30 days**. During this period, you may reactivate your account by logging in. After 30 days, we will permanently delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we are required by law to retain certain records or where data has been incorporated into aggregated, anonymised statistics that do not identify you.

Score and leaderboard history data may be retained in anonymised form for analytical purposes after account deletion.

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or alteration. These measures include encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS), hashed password storage via Firebase Authentication, and access controls limiting who within our organisation can access personal data.

No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and applicable authorities as required by law.

---

## 7. Push Notifications & Emails

TrivioQ may send you mobile and web push notifications, as well as emails, to alert you when a new trivia Drop is available or for system announcements. Push notifications require your explicit permission. You can withdraw permission or manage your notification channels at any time through the app's notification preferences or your device's settings. 

When you grant notification permission, we store your device push token or web push subscription to route notifications. This token is not shared with third parties beyond the notification delivery infrastructure (e.g. Firebase Cloud Messaging).

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
- **Restriction of processing** — request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw at any time.

California residents also have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at **privacy@enatostech.com**. We will respond within 30 days (or sooner where required by law).
